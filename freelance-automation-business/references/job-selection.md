# Job Selection & Pricing

The single biggest lever in this business is *which jobs you bid on*. A mediocre proposal on a
great job beats a great proposal on a bad job. Score first, write second.

## Scoring a posting (0–10, bid at 6+)

Start at 5, then adjust:

**Add points**
- +2 — Task is squarely in the wheelhouse: scraping/scraper repair, spreadsheet automation,
  PDF/data extraction, API glue, small script fixes. (You can deliver same/next day.)
- +1 — "Broken thing needs fixing" framing. Repair jobs close faster and clients are grateful.
- +1 — Client has payment verified AND >4.5★ history AND has hired before.
- +1 — Clear, specific description with sample data/URLs included. Clarity in = clarity out.
- +1 — Budget implies ≥$50/hr at your honest effort estimate.
- +1 — Obvious recurring need (scrapers break, reports repeat monthly) → retainer potential.

**Subtract points**
- −2 — Vague scope ("build me a system", "various tasks", "ongoing developer needed" with no
  first task). These become scope disasters.
- −2 — Budget implies <$25/hr, or "simple job, 5 minutes for an expert" framing.
- −2 — Client history shows disputes, <4★ given to freelancers, or many jobs posted/none hired.
- −1 — More than ~20 proposals already submitted (unless you have a genuine edge).
- −1 — Requires real-time availability, long meetings, or timezone overlap you can't offer
  inside the 1-hour/day constraint.
- **Auto-pass** — asks for free "test tasks" of real work, off-platform payment before a
  contract, account credentials up front, or work that's abusive (spam tooling, review
  manipulation, scraping to violate ToS for resale, credential harvesting).

Output format for each scored job:

```
[7/10 — BID] Fix broken Amazon price scraper — $150 budget
Why: repair job, sample repo linked, verified client 4.9★. Retainer angle: scrapers break.
Suggested bid: $140 fixed, 2-day delivery.
```

## Pricing

- **Fixed price by default.** Hourly punishes you for being fast (and you are fast).
- Anchor to value bands, not hours: trivial fix $75–100; standard script/scraper $100–250;
  multi-source or integration work $250–400; anything bigger, split into milestones.
- Never bid under $50. Sub-$50 clients generate the most support load per dollar.
- Include exactly **one revision round** in the price; further rounds are quoted.
- First 2–3 weeks on a new profile: price ~20% under band to buy the first 5 reviews, and say
  in the proposal that this is an introductory rate. Stop discounting after 5 reviews.

## Portfolio strategy (first 100 days)

Weeks 1–2: bid narrow — one niche (e.g., scraper repair) so reviews compound into search rank
for that keyword. Weeks 3+: expand to adjacent niches only after 5+ reviews. Track which niche
converts best and double down; do not spread bids evenly on principle.
