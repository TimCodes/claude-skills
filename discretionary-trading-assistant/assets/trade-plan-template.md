# Trade Plan Template

Use this exact structure for every actionable (A/B grade) idea. Keep it under a screen — a plan
the trader won't re-read mid-trade is decoration.

---

## Trade Plan — {SYMBOL} {LONG|SHORT} — Grade {A|B}

**{date} {time} — {session context, e.g. "RTH open" / "London/NY overlap"}**

**Setup:** {archetype from playbook} on {timeframe}, context from {higher timeframe}

**Thesis (2–3 sentences):** {why this trade, at this level, now}

**Against (devil's advocate):** {the strongest honest counter-case, 1–2 sentences}

| | Price | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | {price + order type} | {trigger condition} |
| Stop | {price} | {structure it sits behind} |
| Target 1 | {price} | {level it sits in front of} — {R multiple} |
| Target 2 (if any) | {price} | {level} — {R multiple} |

**Size:** {N contracts/shares/lots/units}
`{show the arithmetic: 1R$ ÷ risk per unit = raw → rounded down to N}`

**Risk:** {$ at stop} = {R}. **Heat after entry:** {xR / y%} of {ceiling} {— correlation note if any}

**Data status:** {verified via IBKR/Kraken at {time} | UNVERIFIED — from screenshot/trader}

**Catalysts in hold window:** {none found via news feed | list them | NEWS FEED UNAVAILABLE — unchecked}

**Management:** {when stop moves to breakeven, scale plan, time stop, what kills the trade early}

**Checklist:** sizing arithmetic shown ☐ · heat ≤ ceiling ☐ · margin verified ☐ · no breaker tripped ☐ · calendar checked ☐

**Status: PROPOSED — awaiting trader decision**

---

After the trader's decision, append one line: `DECISION: {taken as planned | modified: how |
passed: why}` and journal the plan per journaling.md regardless of the decision.
