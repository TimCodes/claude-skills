## Tolerances and finish — what to expect

- Dimensional accuracy is approximately **±0.2 mm** on general features. Where a tighter
  fit is needed, say which dimension and it can be reamed or designed with clearance.
- **Layer lines are inherent to the process** and will be visible on all surfaces. Smooth
  injection-moulded finish is achievable only with additional finishing work, quoted
  separately.
- Support-contact surfaces have a rougher texture than free surfaces.
- Colour may vary slightly between filament batches on repeat orders.
